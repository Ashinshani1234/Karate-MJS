02 · Class Experience

Four Pillars of Shotokan & Kobudo Training

Every evening session balances traditional Shotokan fundamentals with modern tournament conditioning and structured belt progression.

Programme 01

Kata (Forms)

Traditional Shotokan choreographed sequences from Taikyoku Shodan and Heian series to Bassai Dai and Kanku Dai—building stance stability, hip snap, and zanshin focus.

Programme 02

Kumite (Sparring)

Distance control (maai), timing, controlled strikes, and WKF competition footwork from Gohon Kumite for beginners up to Jiyu Kumite free sparring.

Programme 03

Nunchaku

Traditional Okinawan weapon discipline developing ambidextrous wrist dexterity, fluid transitions, defensive blocks, and rhythm coordination.

Programme 04

Equipment Training

Makiwara conditioning, heavy kick shields, focus mitts, agility hurdles, and core plyometrics to build power safely across all age brackets.

Selected Training Module

Kata — Shotokan Precision & Breath Control

Students progress systematically through 10 Kyu ranks (White through Brown) before Black Belt (Shodan) examination. Each belt tests Kihon basics, designated Kata, and Ippon/Jiyu Kumite under Sensei Jayasinghe Herath.

White (10th Kyu) Yellow (9th Kyu) Orange (8th Kyu) Green (7th–6th Kyu) Blue (5th–4th Kyu) Brown (3rd–1st Kyu) Black Belt (Instructors · Dan Grade)
